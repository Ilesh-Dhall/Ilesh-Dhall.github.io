This sample page demonstrates how to give a publication a long-form Markdown detail page while keeping the publication list compact.

## Research question

The example asks how a well-structured content model can make research artifacts easier to inspect, cite, and reuse.

## Method

The study combines a documented evaluation protocol, reproducible experiment settings, and links to the associated paper, PDF, and source code.

## Findings

A dedicated detail page gives readers context that does not fit comfortably inside a publication list. The contents navigation is generated from these headings because `show_contents` is enabled in the BibTeX entry.

## Reproducibility

Replace the sample URLs in `content/publications.bib` with the real DOI, paper, PDF, and repository links for your publication.
